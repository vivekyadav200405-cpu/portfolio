A live energy monitoring dashboard built for Toyota Boshoku. Connected and configured energy meters across the plant to capture real-time readings — power consumption, CO₂ emissions, and zone/machine-wise usage. The system highlights which zones and machines are consuming the most energy, helping the team take focused actions for energy saving and sustainability.

  • Live readings from configured energy meters
  • Real-time power consumption tracking
  • CO₂ emission calculation and reporting
  • Zone-wise and machine-wise energy analysis

CMS

Camera Management System – Cycle Time Analyzer

An intelligent system that analyzes part cycles using video clips. It automatically measures the cycle time of each part, generates clip-wise graphs, and highlights only the high-cycle-time operations — so supervisors can focus on the real bottlenecks instead of going through every clip manually.

  • Per-part cycle time measurement from video
  • Auto clip splitting and tagging by cycle
  • Graph visualization of cycle time trends
  • Filter and focus on high cycle-time parts
  • Reduces supervisor analysis time drastically
